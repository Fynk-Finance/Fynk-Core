# "Abonar a esta meta" in the Metas card, with "Deshacer"

## Instruction

With #173 (Metas) and #174 (contribution service) merged, Claude offered to build "Abonar" next and Karol answered "si".

## What was done

* **`ui/AbonarBox.tsx`:** the design's lime box inside an open goal card.
  * "Abonar a esta meta", the chips $50.000, $100.000 and $200.000, "Otro monto" with a "$" prefix, and the lime "Abonar" button. A chip and a typed amount exclude each other.
  * It records today's aporte with `contributionService.add`.
  * The design's "Se descuenta de Bancolombia · Ahorros" is replaced, as the inventory asks, by "Es un registro de lo que apartaste. Fynk no mueve tu dinero."
* **`ui/UndoToast.tsx`:** "Abonaste $100.000 a Viaje a Cartagena · Deshacer" for 5 s, announced to screen readers. "Deshacer" calls `contributionService.undo` and shows "Abono deshecho". New; the design only animates `.fb-toast`.
* **`ui/GoalCard.tsx`:** an `abonar` slot in the open card, between the boxes and "Editar meta".
* **`ui/MetasScreen.tsx`:** takes `contributions` and shows the toast.
* **`index.ts`:** passes the contribution service to Metas.
* **Tests (`ui/MetasScreen.test.tsx`, +6):**
  * The design's box and its "no mueve tu dinero" line.
  * A chip records today's aporte, the card shows $3.300.000 and 66 %, and the toast confirms.
  * A typed amount that completes a goal moves it to "Metas cumplidas".
  * "Deshacer" removes the aporte.
  * No amount records nothing.
  * The toast hides after 5 s.

## Result

* Tests: 107 suites and 1315 tests pass. ESLint, tsc and Prettier are clean.
* Merged locally with #175 (milestones) and #176 (carousel): no conflicts, 113 savings tests and tsc pass together. The Abonar strings sit at the end of the strings object so they do not touch #176's lines.
* Pull request: opened from Karol's account against `develop`.

## Decisions and problems

* **The Abonar box always records today.** The service accepts any past day; a date field belongs to the "Agregar aporte" screen of the M8 prompt (Detalle de meta), which does not exist yet.
* **"Vincular un movimiento" still waits for M7** (`setTransactionGoal` and the movement list).

"Vas a tiempo / Te faltan $X al mes" on the Metas card (SCRUM-53). Then "Detalle de meta" with the list of aportes and a date, and the movement link once M7 exports it.
